# Qwen qualitative-output protocol

Status: frozen on 2026-09-08 before retained execution.

## Purpose

Produce descriptive output comparisons for two task types that accompany,
but do not replace, the quantitative natural-stream endpoints.  Outputs are
generated by Sequential and CAGD models after the complete three-task stream.

## Frozen model and training

- Model: official Qwen3-0.6B snapshot `c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca`.
- Parameter count: 751,632,384 stored parameters; only the final Transformer
  block (15,730,944 parameters) is updated.
- Methods: Sequential and CAGD, seed 3407.
- Training, task order, anchors, generation, and hyperparameters exactly match
  the audited `cagd_natural_qwen_v1` formal runs.  The reproduction is retained
  only if every recorded held-out loss and accuracy differs from its archived
  endpoint by at most `5e-6`.

## Output selection fixed before generation

The following self-contained, moderate-length test examples were selected by
their inputs only, before either reproduced model output was generated:

- Summarization: `summarization:test:0`, `summarization:test:2`, and
  `summarization:test:33`.
- Creative writing: `creative_writing:test:0`, `creative_writing:test:6`, and
  `creative_writing:test:13`.

Generation is greedy with at most 96 new tokens.  Full prompts, references,
and unedited model outputs are retained in JSON.  Paper tables may show a
deterministic prefix for space, explicitly marked as an excerpt.  These tables
are qualitative illustrations and are not used as selected quantitative
evidence.

## Integrity

Retained outputs live under `runs/cagd_qualitative/qwen3_0.6b/`.  Existing
formal runs and their source files remain read-only.  The new runner records
its own hash, this protocol hash, all reused source/data hashes, the archived
endpoint hash, the model inventory, and the maximum metric discrepancy.
